Citizen & Admin, one shared platform

One request, synced everywhere, instantly.

MedNexus connects hospital admins and citizens through a single database — live beds, smart ambulance matching by condition, and a safe, non-prescriptive AI assistant, all in sync.

Two portals, one database

Nothing here is looking at stale data — whatever a citizen requests, the admin sees instantly, and whatever an admin updates, the citizen sees instantly.

CITIZEN PORTAL

Search, book, request, and get guidance

  • Search hospitals by specialty, distance and live availability
  • Book, confirm and track appointments in one flow
  • One-tap emergency call, or a quick request form matched to Normal or Ventilator-support ambulance
  • Care Assistant Bot for safe, non-prescriptive self-care guidance
ADMIN PORTAL

Manage capacity, staff, and requests

  • Live bed tracking — vacant, occupied, ICU — by department
  • On-duty / off-duty staff status and shift scheduling
  • Accept, reschedule or reject appointments from one queue
  • Reports & analytics — inflow trends and emergency alerts
